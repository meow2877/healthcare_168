🩸 Blood Pad

The Blood Pad checks for the presence of blood in urine.

Purpose

Blood in urine may indicate urinary tract infections, kidney stones, kidney disease, or injury to the urinary system.

Normal Result

Negative

Possible Causes of Blood in Urine

  • Kidney Stones
  • Urinary Tract Infection
  • Kidney Disease
  • Bladder Infection
