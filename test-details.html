Laboratory Test Details

Understand the purpose, sample requirements and basic information about commonly performed laboratory tests.

Understanding Your Laboratory Tests

Laboratory tests help healthcare professionals detect, diagnose and monitor different medical conditions. Each test uses a specific type of sample and follows a particular testing procedure.

Complete Blood Count

Purpose

Evaluates the different components of blood including red blood cells, white blood cells, hemoglobin and platelets.

Sample Required

Blood

Helps Detect

  • Anemia
  • Infections
  • Blood disorders

Blood Glucose

Purpose

Measures the amount of glucose present in the blood.

Sample Required

Blood

Helps Detect

  • Diabetes
  • High blood sugar
  • Low blood sugar

Urine Routine Examination

Purpose

Examines urine for physical, chemical and microscopic abnormalities.

Sample Required

Urine

Helps Detect

  • Urinary infections
  • Kidney problems
  • Abnormal urine composition

Hemoglobin Test

Purpose

Measures the amount of hemoglobin in the blood, which carries oxygen throughout the body.

Sample Required

Blood

Helps Detect

  • Anemia
  • Low hemoglobin
  • Other blood-related conditions

Lipid Profile

Purpose

Measures different types of cholesterol and triglycerides in the blood.

Sample Required

Blood

Helps Assess

  • Cholesterol levels
  • Triglycerides
  • Heart disease risk

Liver Function Test

Purpose

Measures substances in the blood that provide information about liver function.

Sample Required

Blood

Helps Assess

  • Liver function
  • Liver injury
  • Related disorders

Common Sample Types

Different laboratory tests require different biological samples. Common samples include:

  • Blood samples
  • Urine samples
  • Swab samples
  • Other specialized biological samples
