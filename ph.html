🧪 pH Pad

The pH Pad measures the acidity or alkalinity of urine.

Purpose

Urine pH helps diagnose urinary tract infections, kidney disorders, and metabolic conditions.

Normal Range

pH 4.5 – 8.0

Abnormal pH May Indicate

  • Urinary Tract Infection
  • Kidney Stones
  • Kidney Disorders
  • Dietary Changes
