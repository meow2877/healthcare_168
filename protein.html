🧪 Protein Pad

The Protein Pad detects the presence of protein in urine.

Purpose

Protein is normally absent or present only in very small amounts in urine. High levels may indicate kidney disease, kidney infection, or other medical conditions.

Normal Result

Negative or Trace Protein

Possible Causes of High Protein

  • Kidney Disease
  • Urinary Tract Infection
  • High Blood Pressure
  • Diabetes
