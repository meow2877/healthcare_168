⚡ Ketone Pad

The Ketone Pad detects ketones in urine.

Purpose

Ketones are produced when the body burns fat instead of glucose for energy. High ketone levels may indicate uncontrolled diabetes or prolonged fasting.

Normal Result

Negative

Possible Causes of Ketones

  • Diabetes
  • Starvation
  • Low-Carbohydrate Diet
  • Prolonged Vomiting
